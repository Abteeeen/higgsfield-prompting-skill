## Practical adoption

Before a later authorized test, prepare a small table assigning each uploaded asset its actual interface handle, intended role and excluded attributes. Resolve contradictions against one shot plan. For a motion reference, distinguish path, placement and cut boundaries from permitted appearance, acting and effect changes. A precise match cut needs matched compositions, not only matching prose. Keep alternate recipes separate from the final assembly, and time dialogue against a real performance.
