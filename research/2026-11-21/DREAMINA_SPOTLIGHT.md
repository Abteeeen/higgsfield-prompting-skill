The announcement ends the program on December 31, 2026. It describes Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok entries, with no required content category. Videos must be at least 30 seconds, show what was made with Dreamina, tag `@dreamina_ai`, and include `#dreamina` and `#dreaminacreators`. Views, likes and comments must remain publicly visible.

It specifies organic views, a view check seven days after publication, review/verification, one highest tier per approved post rather than stacked tiers, up to ten posts per creator per month, and a $50,000 monthly cap. Cash settlement is described as every two weeks after review, with taxes/fees borne by the creator. To receive both cash and credits for a breakout tier, the corresponding reusable workflow/template must also be published on Dreamina.

The carousel directs creators to Discord and a submission form through QR codes. Those destinations and the complete eligibility terms have not yet been reviewed. Outstanding questions include eligible countries, residency/payment requirements, submission deadlines, cross-post view aggregation, whether mixed-platform footage qualifies, and how reused trend concepts are reviewed.

Higgsfield tests are production experiments. Do not label a Higgsfield-only export as a qualifying Dreamina entry. Preserve Dreamina generation IDs, source assets, workflow and publication timestamps for any intended entry.
