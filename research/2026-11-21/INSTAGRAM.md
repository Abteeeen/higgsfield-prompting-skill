# Instagram review ledger

Observed 2026-11-21. Engagement is a snapshot, not audited organic reach. Rounded views came from Instagram's reels grids unless stated otherwise. Likes/comments came from public post metadata. Some posts use keyword-comment gates, so comment totals are not independent endorsements.

For the 20 reels below, the complete caption and available full-length speech transcript were read; visual contact sheets covered the whole timeline at 2–3-second intervals. This is **not a claim of frame-by-frame or uninterrupted playback review**. Small on-screen prompts sometimes remain unreadable or are hidden behind a comment gate. No comments or DMs were sent to obtain gated material. Two reels are explicitly excluded as complete tutorials. The requested 20 popular, fully analyzed Instagram guides remain unfinished.

## What to carry into production

There is no single platform-specific prose trick. The chosen **mode and attached references** change what the same words mean. A short template replacement instruction cannot replace a new-scene shot plan. An image-generation prompt that defines appearance should not be copied unchanged into a motion-only edit. Treat gated demonstrations as ideas until their missing steps are resolved.
