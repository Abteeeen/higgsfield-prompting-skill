## What to carry into production

There is no single platform-specific prose trick. The chosen **mode and attached references** change what the same words mean. A short template replacement instruction cannot replace a new-scene shot plan. An image-generation prompt that defines appearance should not be copied unchanged into a motion-only edit. Treat gated demonstrations as ideas until their missing steps are resolved.
