Date: 2026-11-21. **Draft for discussion, not approval to generate or publish.** The user explicitly asked to agree the strategy before making videos. All production is on hold.

November 22 evidence update: the four candidate metrics below retain their November 21 observation dates. The November 14 transformation has aged out of the current trailing week. ## Recommendation

Build around timely **formats**, without committing to a subject niche. Separate learning how a tool works from deciding what audiences currently want. Use current engagement to select a small number of formats, analyze their story mechanics, then develop original variations. Treat prompting as a repeatable testing discipline rather than a collection of magic phrases.

Our strongest verified recent candidate is [Rezai Studio's hidden-worker story](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df9p5sCxV2Z/), published November 20 and observed at 72.5M views on November 21, then **89.3M on November 22**. The second live grid observation supports continued uptake of this particular post; it does not establish a precise growth rate, category-wide performance or likely remake results. Its useful mechanism is an immediately surprising situation, a sympathetic character, a concrete need, costly effort and a visible payoff. See the [full analysis and dated counters](research/2026-11-21/VIRAL_CANDIDATES.md).

A second direction is [Kaye's dinosaurs in everyday life](https://www.instagram.com/kaye.creatives/reel/Df6Ho3VSBXy/), published November 19 and observed at 2.2M views. It suggests a lighter format built on familiar routines with an impossible companion. Although the reel offers a guide through comments, a [public creator-blog recipe has since been reviewed](research/2026-11-22/KAYE_GUIDES.md), including all seven scene recipes and sixteen reference figures. Its correspondence with the reel is strong, but exact original run provenance and the reason for its reach remain unknown. It also comes from a much larger account. Compare these directions before choosing a concept; neither is approved for production.

## Decision brief

For the next story discussion, put **E1 — The Last Light** first on the current evidence. **T1 — The Last Ticket Booth** remains a historical format option, but its supporting November 14 post has aged out of the current week; obtain a fresh qualifying example before treating it as a current trend choice. Compare surreal companionship and physical comedy as alternatives, without fixing the account's niche. These judgments come from a four-post sample, not a proven ranking of formats or a forecast of our results.

| Direction | Proposed original concept | First technical question, only after authorization | What success would establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emotional story | E1: firefly leaves its lamp to guide a lost child | Can one tiny moving light remain the same creature while the child follows in readable geography? | Feasibility of the story's hardest interaction; not emotional impact or likely views |
| Progressive transformation | T1: abandoned ticket booth becomes a nighttime library | Can the furnishings change while the booth's dimensions, doorway and window stay fixed? | Feasibility of matching transformation stages; not a complete film or physical construction plan |
| Surreal companion | C1: a pet raincloud learns to water a flower | Can rain stay over the pot without drifting onto the nearby person? | Localized action control with the chosen references |
| Physical comedy | K2: overequipped robot goalkeeper misses a small ball | Can the ball pass visibly between two stationary boots without intersecting or disappearing? | Readability of the gag's decisive action |

The sequence is **story choice → text storyboard → test of that story's hardest interaction → complete story production → release review**. A technically easy test in a different format does not validate the chosen film. Keep the first test small, preserve its exact inputs and inspect the whole output before expanding. Current authorization stops at text planning.

After publishing is separately authorized, judge technical and audience results separately. A clean render can still have a weak hook; a high-view reference does not supply our account's retention data. Compare openings at equal elapsed ages, use account analytics where available, and decide on another iteration from observed audience behavior. Dreamina reward eligibility is a third independent check, not a consequence of either technical quality or reach.

## 1. Select trends by evidence

A third current option is [football mishap and reaction comedy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df0eWO5x9i4/), observed at 11M views. Instagram's post timestamp is November 17 UTC, displayed locally as November 18; the metadata disagreement is recorded in the ledger. Its escalating player/coach reactions offer a different format from the two narrative examples. The 21-second Higgsfield-credited clip is a research reference, not evidence of Spotlight eligibility. An original fictional-team variation would need its own concept, source material and payoff before production approval.

For each research session, collect a dated sample from Instagram across emotional animation, visual transformations, comedy, surreal everyday scenes and other emerging formats. These are search categories, not a permanent content niche.

A fourth observed option is [a concealed-home transformation](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfvVinmSEN-/), with 25.4M live grid views and a November 14 23:00 UTC timestamp, near this week's boundary. Its 82.5-second sequence combines neighbor conflict with successive construction reveals. Audience comments also question physical plausibility, so engagement cannot be equated with admiration or instructional accuracy. Three transformation concepts now cover this fourth direction in the concept comparison. Exact prompts/model and independent replication remain unverified.

Each candidate record should include:

- Original URL and creator; exact publication date if available.
- Views, likes, comments and visible shares/reposts, with observation time and whether counts are rounded.
- Follower count as context; do not assume followers equal the audience reached.
- First-second hook, sequence of events, payoff, duration and sound dependency.
- Whether it is a tutorial, ordinary creator post, sponsored demonstration or model launch.
- Production requirements and what we would change to make an original version.

Use the trailing seven days for the current shortlist. Older examples remain technique references. Revisit promising posts for a second count before claiming growth speed: a single snapshot cannot measure acceleration. Compare several independent creators before calling something a repeatable trend. A repost of the same original does not count as independent success.

Apply that rule to the actual media, not just different usernames. Our [historical camcorder audit](research/2026-11-21/REDDIT.md#camcorder-example-source-tracing-and-adaptation) traced a highly voted Reddit clip to matching footage in an earlier 12.9M-view X post. A separate rendition changed several story beats, but its prompt and video view count were unavailable. Record the earliest located source, distinguish reposts from adaptations, and compare the same engagement measure before claiming repeatable reach.

Prioritize candidates with all three: recent reach, a clearly understood mechanism, and an achievable original adaptation. Raw views alone do not decide. Public metrics cannot establish organic reach or guarantee Dreamina approval.

## 2. Learn prompting in distinct workflows

| Workflow | What the prompt must do | What the interface/reference supplies |
|---|---|---|
| New scene in Seedance | Visible actions, staging, shot order, sound and ending | Identity and environment references; actual duration/aspect/resolution controls |
| Edit an existing video | Identify the requested change and preservation priorities | Source performance, timing and scene structure |
| Dreamina Video Recreator template | Specify the replacement or adaptation | Template motion or composition; selected Motion Transfer or Creative Remix mode |
| Image-to-video | Explain what starts moving and how the scene ends | The approved starting composition and appearance |

The tutorials repeatedly use reference preparation, explicit role assignment and staged edits. They do not establish a universal best word count, JSON schema or exact timestamp reliability. Use [CURRENT_WORKFLOW.md](CURRENT_WORKFLOW.md) for the practical method and the source ledgers for disagreements.

After production is explicitly authorized, improve through a small test log: one hypothesis, fixed references/settings, one changed variable, complete output review, then a documented decision. Do not spend on a final render merely because a draft looks impressive in its first frame.

## 3. Develop concepts before generating

For each shortlisted format, prepare three concise concept variations on paper. Change the central situation, characters, causal sequence and payoff—not just names or colors. Select one by hook clarity, emotional or visual payoff, feasibility and originality.

The [concept comparison](CONCEPT_OPTIONS.md) now provides twelve original options, three per observed direction, and four provisional paper finalists with six-beat outlines. Each finalist has an asset list and a test tied to its hardest interaction. These are creative judgments for discussion, not performance forecasts or a production selection.

Current discussion candidate: **The Last Light**. An exhausted firefly powers a village streetlamp, leaves it to guide a lost child home, and receives kindness in return. This is an untested concept inspired by a broad narrative mechanism. It has not been selected by the user, and no successful generation exists. The earlier draft prompt is retained only as a draft.

Do not prematurely commit the account to this style. The next well-supported trend may belong to another category. The research shortlist currently needs more recent independent examples before a weekly production slate is defensible.

## 4. Plan a measurable release cycle, after approval

Prepare the story beats and final payoff first. Then create consistent assets, generate shots, edit, review and publish only within the user's eventual authorization. Test in Higgsfield using verified web entitlement; create intended reward entries with the required Dreamina provenance once eligibility is resolved.

Review performance at consistent intervals such as 24 hours, 72 hours and seven days. These are proposed checkpoints, not scheduled automations. Record views, audience retention/watch time when the account exposes them, shares/saves and follower conversion. Compare against our own account baseline and similar-duration posts. Use results to decide whether to refine a format, try another hook or move on.

Do not set universal retention or view thresholds before we have account data. Do not delete a post merely because it starts slowly, and do not count purchased distribution toward an organic-view reward.

Trial Reels are a possible way to compare openings after publishing is authorized and account availability is verified. Start with two materially different openings, compare at equal elapsed ages and keep other controllable factors constant. This is a proposed experiment, not a guaranteed growth technique. The inspected creator demonstration changes both hooks and captions and supplies no controlled comparison; its automation remains gated. See the [Trial Reels evidence and experiment](research/2026-11-21/TRIAL_REELS.md), including unresolved Dreamina treatment of trials and repeat variants.

Its announcement requires videos of at least 30 seconds, specified tags and review. The official submission form restricts publication to November/December 2026 and groups cross-post URLs in one response per creative work. It does not confirm view aggregation. Full eligibility and submission deadlines remain unresolved; see [program evidence](research/2026-11-21/DREAMINA_SPOTLIGHT.md).

Before committing a production slate to the reward, confirm country/payment eligibility, submission timing, reuse rules, Dreamina provenance and how platform views are counted. Reconcile participant-specific tags and payout timing against the latest official creator notices; the public carousel alone is not the account's complete checklist. Keep private correspondence outside this public repository, and keep cash earnings separate from non-cash credits. A popular Higgsfield-only video cannot simply be relabeled as a Dreamina entry.

The user will provide Dreamina account access later. Leave account-dependent checks open and continue public-source research and concept planning in the meantime; do not repeatedly request the account or infer eligibility from the delay.
